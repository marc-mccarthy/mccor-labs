## The short version

We collect what you send us through the contact form, and nothing else. We do
not use cookies. We do not use analytics. We do not track you across websites,
and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising.

The detail below explains this more precisely, because you should not have to
take our word for it.

## What we collect, and when

### When you use the contact form

The form asks for:

- **Your name** — required
- **Your email address** — required, so we can reply
- **Your company** — optional
- **Your budget range** — optional
- **Your message** — required

We use this only to respond to your enquiry and, if it leads somewhere, to
carry on that conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list. We do not have
a mailing list.

The form includes a hidden field that automated bots fill in and people do not.
If it is filled in, we discard the submission. It collects nothing about you.

### When you email us directly

We receive whatever you send, including your email address and anything in your
signature or attachments. Same use: replying to you.

### When you simply read the site

Nothing is collected in your browser. Specifically:

- **No cookies are set.** Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for
  functionality
- **No analytics service is used.** We do not know how many people visit this
  site or which pages they read
- **No advertising or tracking pixels**
- **No embedded third-party scripts.** Fonts are served from our own domain
  rather than a font CDN, so no third party sees your request for them
- **No social media widgets or share buttons that phone home**

If you have turned on a "do not track" or "global privacy control" signal,
there is nothing here for it to switch off.

The site does store a small amount of information _in your own browser_ to
remember your preferences — for example, that you have already seen the intro
animation, or whether you turned sound on or off. This never leaves your device,
is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time through your browser
settings.

### Server logs

We do not keep server access logs for this website.

## Who else sees your information

We keep this list short on purpose.

| Who                                          | What they get                                                                                     | Why                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amazon Web Services**                      | Contact form submissions in transit and at rest; the email itself                                 | They host this site and run the email delivery. Data is processed in the US East (N. Virginia) region |
| **Google** (Calendar appointment scheduling) | Whatever you enter when you book a call — typically your name, email address and any note you add | Only if you click through to book. Google's own privacy policy applies from that point                |

That is the complete list. We do not use a CRM, an email marketing platform, an
analytics provider, an advertising network, or a chat widget.

**We have never sold personal information and will not.** We do not "share" it
for cross-context behavioural advertising, in the sense those terms are used in
US state privacy laws.

**Booking a call is a deliberate handoff.** The booking button is an ordinary
link to a third-party scheduling service — we do not embed their code in this
site, so they see nothing about you until you choose to click it. Once you are
on their page, you are subject to their privacy policy, not this one.

## How long we keep it

Contact form submissions and email correspondence are kept for **[24] months**
from our last exchange, then deleted. If your enquiry becomes a project, the
correspondence is kept as a business record for as long as we are required to
keep it.

Ask us to delete your information sooner and we will, unless we are legally
required to keep it.

## How it is protected

- All traffic to this site is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- Contact form submissions are encrypted in transit
- Our email is stored on encrypted storage, and access requires
  multi-factor authentication
- Only the people who need to see an enquiry can see it — this is a four-person
  firm, so that is a short list

No system is perfectly secure, and we are not going to claim otherwise. What we
can say is that we do not collect what we do not need, which is the most
effective protection available.

## Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to:

- **Tell you what we hold** about you
- **Correct it** if it is wrong
- **Delete it**
- **Send you a copy** in a portable format
- **Stop using it**

Email [marcm@mccorlabs.com](mailto:marcm@mccorlabs.com) and we will respond
within 30 days. We will not charge you and we will not make it difficult.

If you are in the **European Economic Area or the United Kingdom**, you also
have the right to object to processing and to complain to your national data
protection authority. Our lawful basis for handling your enquiry is legitimate
interest — you contacted us and we are replying.

If you are a **California** resident, you have the rights described above under
the CCPA as amended. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is
no opt-out to exercise. We will not discriminate against you for making a
request.

If you are a **Minnesota** resident, the Minnesota Consumer Data Privacy Act
gives you comparable rights. We will honour them regardless of whether the Act's
size thresholds apply to a firm of this size.

## Children

This website is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We do not
knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has
sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

## International visitors

We operate in the United States and your information is processed there. If you
are contacting us from outside the US, you are sending it to the US by doing so.

## Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect — adding analytics, for instance, or a new tool
that handles your information — we will update this page and change the date at
the top. Material changes will be flagged on the page rather than made quietly.

## Complaints

Email [marcm@mccorlabs.com](mailto:marcm@mccorlabs.com) first. We would rather
sort it out directly. If you are in the EEA or UK and we do not resolve it, you
can complain to your national data protection authority. If you are in the US,
your state attorney general's office handles privacy complaints.

## Notes for phase 2

- This page is one of the two routes with no signature moment. Long-form
  reading: generous line height, a measure of 65–75 characters, clear heading
  hierarchy. Resist decorating it.
- Exactly one `h1` ("Privacy Policy"), no skipped heading levels — the
  accessibility suite checks legal routes like any other.
- The "Last updated" date should be a real date, not generated at build time —
  a date that silently changes on every deploy is worse than no date.
- The bracketed placeholders are deliberate. They must be filled or removed
  before launch; `CONTENT-TODO.md` should list each one, and phase 4's
  definition of done requires that file to be empty.
- **Keep this page honest as the site changes.** Adding analytics means editing
  the "what we collect" section and the third-party table in the same commit.
  A stale privacy policy is a worse liability than a cautious one.
